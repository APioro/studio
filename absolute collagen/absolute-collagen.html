Absolute Collagen

As part of a university collaboration with Absolute Collagen’s design team, I was tasked with redesigning their mobile webshop to enhance usability and visual coherence. The project involved identifying key UX issues, defining the scope, and prioritizing improvements that would have the greatest impact. This project was about refining and clarifying an already strong brand rather than starting from scratch.
